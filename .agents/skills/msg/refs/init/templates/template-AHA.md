# AHA — Institutional Knowledge Log

Past mistakes and learnings that should inform future product planning.

Each entry is **2–3 lines total — one terse clause per field, no narrative, no
restated context** (`script-aha.sh` rejects any field over 140 chars):
- **Summary**: one-line description of the mistake or learning
- **Why**: root cause — why it happened
- **Note**: what to watch for or do differently next time
