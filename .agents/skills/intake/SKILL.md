  The planning front-door. Captures feature ideas and bugs as graded rows in
  the root INTAKE.md ledger. Use it when the user says "log an idea",
  "capture a bug", "add to the backlog", "note this down", "track this
  feature", or invokes `$intake`. Owns the requirements interview — fleshes
  out thin ideas, proactively suggests adjacent ideas, splits
  compound/hybrid asks and ideas grading C:8 or higher into discrete rows,
  and grades every idea in a single-turn banded judgment (complexity /
  token-cost / sequencing). Feeds `plan-pm`, which drafts the PRD. Also owns
  `--update`: use it when the user says "update that idea", "change the goal
  on #4", "edit the backlog row about search", or "fix the idea I logged" —
  it lists the un-shipped rows and edits one in place. And `--delete`: use
  it when the user says "delete that idea", "remove row #4", "drop that from
  the backlog", or "I logged that by mistake" — it warns about what the
  removal breaks, then removes the row on confirmation.
